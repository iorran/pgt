# Instructor Guide

Complete guide to managing your BJJ academy with PGT.

> [!info] Related guides
> - [[User Guide - Student|Student Guide]]

## Getting Started

### Create Your Academy

1. Sign up at the login page → click **"Sign Up"**
2. Choose **"Create Academy"**
3. Fill in your academy name and city
4. You become the instructor and owner of the new academy

### Set Your Gym Location

This is required for proximity-based check-ins to work.

1. Open the sidebar → **Settings** (Configurações)
2. While at the gym, tap **"Use my current location"**
3. Your browser will ask for location permission — approve it
4. Coordinates are saved

### Share Your Join Code

Students need a join code to register at your academy.

1. Open the **Dashboard** (Painel)
2. The join code appears in your academy card (e.g., `PGT-PONTINHA-CM7`)
3. Tap **"Copy"** or **"Share on WhatsApp"** to send it

## Managing Students

### Approve New Students

When a student joins with your code, they appear as **Pending**.

1. Sidebar → **Students** → **Pending** tab
2. Tap **Approve** or **Reject** for each student
3. Approved students gain access to the academy features

### View Student Details

1. Sidebar → **Students** → **Active** tab
2. Click any student to see their profile, belt, payment history, and membership

### Assign a Membership Plan

1. Open a student's detail page
2. Click **"Assign Membership"**
3. Pick a plan from the dropdown, set start date and due day (day of month)
4. Save

## Classes

### Create a Class

1. Sidebar → **Classes**
2. Tap **"Create Class"**
3. Fill in: name, type (Gi/No-Gi/Open Mat/Kids), days of week (multi-select), start and end time
4. A separate class is created for each day you select

### Edit or Delete a Class

- On any class card, tap the **pencil icon** to edit
- Tap the **trash icon** to delete (soft delete — historical check-ins are preserved)

### View Check-in History

1. Sidebar → **Classes** → **History** tab
2. See all student check-ins ordered by date

## Check-in System

Students can check in to classes two ways:

### Method 1: Proximity Button

Students tap a button on their phone. The system verifies they're within 250 meters of your gym (using GPS) and that the class is currently active (15 min before to 1 hour after the scheduled time).

### Method 2: QR Code Totem

The QR code totem turns any tablet into a check-in station.

1. Open `/totem` on a tablet at your gym entrance
2. The page automatically shows QR codes for all currently active classes
3. Codes refresh every 5 minutes
4. Students scan with their phone camera → check-in completes automatically

> [!tip]
> Leave the totem page open all day. It auto-detects which classes are active based on the schedule.

## Billing

### Create Membership Plans

1. Sidebar → **Billing** → **Plans** tab
2. Tap **"Create Plan"**
3. Fill in: name, price, frequency (monthly/quarterly/yearly), classes per week
4. Save

### Edit Plans

Click the **pencil icon** on any plan card to update price or details.

### Record a Payment

1. Sidebar → **Billing** → **Payments** tab
2. Tap **"Record Payment"**
3. Select student, amount, payment date, and reference month (which month the payment covers)
4. Save

### Track Overdue Students

1. Sidebar → **Billing** → **Overdue** tab
2. Students with unpaid memberships past their due day appear here
3. Cards are color-coded: yellow (1-7 days late) or red (8+ days late)

## Notifications

### Notification Bell

The bell icon in the top-right header shows a red badge with the number of overdue students.

Click the bell to see the dropdown with each overdue student. For each one you can:

- **Send Reminder** — opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled payment reminder message
- **Send Email** — sends a payment reminder email automatically
- **Mute** (bell-off icon) — hides the student from the notification list (use for special arrangements)

### Mute a Student

If a student has a special arrangement (e.g., helping clean the gym in exchange for a discount), tap the mute icon in the notification bell to stop seeing them in your overdue list.

## Marketplace

### Add Products

1. Sidebar → **Marketplace** → **Products** tab
2. Tap **"Add Product"**
3. Fill in name, description, price, stock
4. Save

### Manage Orders

1. Sidebar → **Marketplace** → **Orders** tab
2. See all student orders with status (pending, confirmed, delivered, cancelled)
3. Tap **"Deliver"** to mark an order as delivered

## Gamification

### Create a Season

1. Sidebar → **Gamification** → **Seasons** tab
2. Tap **"Create Season"**
3. Set name, start date, end date, and points awarded for 1st/2nd/3rd place

### Approve Competition Results

When students submit results, you review them.

1. Sidebar → **Gamification** → **Results** tab
2. Pending results appear at the top
3. Tap **Approve** to award points or **Reject** to decline

### View Leaderboard

Sidebar → **Gamification** → **Leaderboard** tab. Filter by adults/kids and belt level.

## Tournaments

1. Sidebar → **Tournaments**
2. Tap **"Create Tournament"** to add upcoming events
3. View signed-up roster for each tournament
4. Students can sign up themselves with their weight class

## Tips

- **Set the gym location first** — proximity check-ins won't work without it
- **Leave the totem open on a tablet** — it's the easiest check-in method for students
- **Use WhatsApp reminders** — students respond much faster than to email
- **Check the dashboard daily** — quick stats overview
- **The notification bell** — your daily payment-management workflow lives here
