# Student Guide

Everything you need to know about using PGT to track your BJJ training.

> [!info] Related guides
> - [[User Guide - Instructor|Instructor Guide]]

## Getting Started

### Join Your Academy

You need a join code from your instructor (e.g., `PGT-PONTINHA-CM7`).

1. Go to the app and tap **"Sign Up"**
2. Choose **"Join with code"**
3. Enter the code your instructor sent you
4. Fill in your details: name, email, password, phone, date of birth, belt
5. Submit

### Wait for Approval

After signing up, your status is **Pending**. Your instructor needs to approve you before you can access classes. You'll see a waiting screen until then.

## Checking In to Classes

You can check in two ways. Both are equally valid.

### Method 1: Tap the Check-in Button

1. Sidebar → **Classes**
2. For any class that's currently active, you'll see a **"Check-in"** button
3. Tap it — your phone will ask for location permission, approve it
4. The system verifies you're at the gym (within 250m) and logs your attendance

> [!warning] You must be physically at the gym
> The system uses your phone's GPS. If you're not within 250 meters of the gym, the check-in is rejected.

### Method 2: Scan the QR Code

If your gym has a tablet at the entrance showing QR codes:

1. Open your phone camera
2. Point it at the QR code for the class you're attending
3. Tap the link that appears
4. The check-in completes automatically

### Check-in Rules

- You can only check in to a class **once per day**
- The class must be **currently happening** (15 min before to 1 hour after the scheduled time)
- You can't check in to two classes that start at the same time
- If you forgot to check in, ask your instructor — they can record it manually

### View Your Check-in History

Sidebar → **Classes** → **History** tab. See all your past check-ins.

## Payments

### Payment Banners

When you log in, the dashboard shows a banner if:

- **Your payment is due soon** (3 days before): orange banner
- **Your payment is overdue**: red banner showing days late

### Payment History

Open your profile to see all recorded payments — date, amount, and which month they cover.

> [!note] You can't pay through the app
> The app only tracks payments. Pay your instructor however you normally do (cash, transfer, etc.) — they'll record it in the system.

## Gamification

### Your Profile

Sidebar → **Gamification** → **Profile** tab. See your:

- Total XP
- Current streak (consecutive weeks of training)
- Longest streak
- Badges earned

### Submit Competition Results

When you compete in a tournament, submit your result for points.

1. Sidebar → **Gamification** → **Results** tab
2. Tap **"Submit Result"**
3. Fill in: season, tournament name, your position (1st/2nd/3rd)
4. Submit — your instructor will review and approve it

### Leaderboard

Sidebar → **Gamification** → **Leaderboard** tab. See where you rank against other students. Filter by belt level.

## Marketplace

### Browse Products

Sidebar → **Marketplace**. See gear, kimonos, and other items your academy sells.

### Place an Order

1. Tap any product card
2. Tap **"Request"**
3. Your instructor receives the order and prepares it
4. Track status under the **Orders** tab

## Tournaments

### Sign Up for an Event

1. Sidebar → **Tournaments**
2. Find the tournament you want to attend
3. Tap **"Sign Up"** and select your weight class
4. You're on the roster

## Tips

- **Allow location access** when prompted — required for proximity check-ins
- **Check in every time you train** — it builds your streak and XP
- **Submit competition results** — get recognized on the leaderboard
- **Watch for payment reminders** — the orange banner tells you when payment is due
