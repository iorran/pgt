# BJJ Academy App

A web application for Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu academies focused on solving class management, payment tracking, and revenue generation.

## Vision

Empower academy owners/instructors to manage their school efficiently — know who's training, who's paying, and grow revenue through merchandise and tournaments.

## Primary User

Academy owner/instructor.

Students have limited access: check-in to pre-registered classes and receive payment alerts.

## Key Pain Points (Why We're Building This)

- Billing is a mess — no structured tracking of who owes what
- No visibility into class attendance
- Students forget to pay (payments are manual bank transfers, not in-app)
- Instructor already sells gear and organizes tournaments but has no tooling for it

## Strategy

- Build for any academy, but **pilot at our own academy first**
- Web app (easiest to ship and covers the needs)
- Students don't pay through the app — they transfer manually to the instructor
